How sequence records are matched

The explorer searches iNaturalist fungal observations that have a value in the DNA Barcode ITS observation field. ITS refers to the internal transcribed spacer region commonly used as a fungal DNA barcode.

“Show only sequences with matches” shows observations whose normalized ITS value occurs on more than one loaded observation.

These are matches between values entered in iNaturalist's observation field. A match does not independently verify the sequence, specimen, or species identification. Follow the links to iNaturalist to review the original observation and its field data.
